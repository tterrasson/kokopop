The sun rose over the misty hills, painting the sky with gold.
Birds sang their morning choruses as the forest awoke.
A narrow trail wound through ancient pines, whispering secrets.
I tightened my boots and inhaled the crisp, pine‑scented air.
Soon, a brook babbled nearby, its water crystal clear.
I paused to watch minnows darting beneath the surface.
A deer emerged from the shadows, pausing to glance.
Its amber eyes met mine for a heartbeat, then it fled.
Further ahead, an abandoned stone cottage stood.
Vines claimed the roof, and moss covered the walls.
Inside, a faded carpet hinted at stories long forgotten.
I imagined generations gathering around a hearth.
The wind rustled the old curtains, echoing soft lullabies.
Sunlight filtered through cracked windows, casting patterns.
I felt a profound peace, as if time itself softened.
The path climbed steeper, revealing a valley below.
Fields of lavender stretched toward the horizon.
Bees hummed, dancing among violet blossoms.
At the valley's edge, a lone oak tree watched the sunset.
Its silhouette against the amber sky was a perfect ending.
Night fell, and fireflies lit the meadow like floating stars.
